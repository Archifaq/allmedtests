Allergy and sensitivity guides cover tests used to explore whether symptoms may be linked to immune responses to allergens such as foods, pollen, mold, animal dander, medicines, or contact exposures.

Common approaches include skin testing and blood tests that measure allergen-specific IgE, with the choice depending on symptoms, history, and the clinical setting. This category is educational only while provider and test catalog data is under review.
