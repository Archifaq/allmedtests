## How to use Spectrophotometer?

Here is an easy step by step guide to using Spectrophotometer in your lab.

1. The first step will be turning the device on and let it heat for some time. Heating the device ensures the better performance during the experiment.
2. A good think about the device is that you can set up the desired wavelength. Since it is necessary for the test, so do it first.
3. Transmittance must be kept at zero in the beginning and you can do it by using the dial built on the left.
4. Apparatus must be kept clean. You will need to wipe down the cuvette from the outside. Do that by wiping it with distilled water.
5. Then put the cuvette in the compartment of the cell.
6. In the sixth step, the transmittance is needed to be at 100% and you can do by doing the opposite of 3rd step. Just use the dial to the right and raise the transmittance up to 100%.
7. Now instead of the blank, put the cuvette in the cell compartment which contains the sample.
8. Now everything is set and ready to measure the absorbance and transmittance. Take the readings carefully and your experiment must be a successful one.
